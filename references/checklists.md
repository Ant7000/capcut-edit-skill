# Checklists

## Before cutting
- [ ] The take list is exactly the files they named. Anything else: ask.
- [ ] Every file's sound profiled; odd ones flagged before use.
- [ ] Phone footage checked for HDR and frame rate.
- [ ] If a project exists: looked at it as it is now, and noted their hand edits.

## After the first cut
- [ ] `tighten_gaps.py` run.
- [ ] `selfcheck_cut.py` with the script: no doubled or clipped words, no silences over half a second, missing lines listed as pickups.
- [ ] Repeats inside numbers and names intact.
- [ ] Ad-libs list read; throat clears cut.
- [ ] Every number said is right; wrong ones listed as pickups.

## Before any write to CapCut
- [ ] CapCut quit (`capcut_app.py status`); if not, told them and offered to quit it.
- [ ] Dry run read; crossing notes understood.
- [ ] Their yes for big changes.
- [ ] Afterwards: clip count and length checked, backup named, checker run, offered to reopen CapCut.

## Before saying graphics are ready
- [ ] Every word on screen is a word they said; numbers right.
- [ ] Each graphic lands on its word.
- [ ] `check_placement.py`: no FLASH, CLASH or LONG; NEAR and SLIVER explained.
- [ ] Density about what their style guide asks.
- [ ] Their look, not the placeholder.
- [ ] Stills checked by me, then by them, before video.

## Before upload
- [ ] Their music pass done.
- [ ] Loudness measured on the final file, within 0.5 of the target.
- [ ] Subtitles made from that exact file, mishears fixed.

## End of a session
- [ ] `log.md` in the video folder says what changed, the backups, and what's next.
- [ ] Every correction they made is in `lessons.md` or the style guide.
